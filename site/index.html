The band spans three scenarios for who runs stake without a named operator. Below ten (shaded), a handful of firms can together stall finality or choose which history to keep.

Are we on the right track? It has fallen from {{N50_PEAK_LOW}} in {{PEAK_MONTH}} to {{N50_LOW}}, inside the danger zone since {{BELOW_SINCE}}. Ethereum's development should treat arresting the slide as a goal in its own right, and find ways to make running validators simpler and more rewarding for independent operators than for the largest firms. Changes on the horizon could make it worse: issuance cuts squeeze thin-margin operators first, post-quantum signatures make every validator heavier to run, and AI raises operational-security and supply-chain risk, making security harder and pushing stake towards the few large firms that can afford to spend heavily on it.
